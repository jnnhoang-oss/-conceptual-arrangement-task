Conceptual Arrangement Task

In this task, you will click and drag images around the screen. Move all images into the circular arena and place conceptually similar items closer together.

After one round of arrangement, you might notice that you form several clusters of similar images. Please remember both the distances within the clusters and between the clusters are significant.

For example: if you have images of a circle, an oval, and a square, you might place the circle and oval closer together, while placing the square farther away from both.
